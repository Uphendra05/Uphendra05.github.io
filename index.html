About

Building games through code & creativity.

Howdy! I'm Uphendhra Nagarajan, a Game Programmer with a deep love for game development and a versatile skill set. I've been making games right from my teenage. I compete in game jams and make personal projects and try to acquire knowledge every time I develop a game. Graduated from Fanshawe College, Canada in Game Development and Software Testing. Alongside gameplay and systems programming, I developed a strong focus on Graphics Programming , enhancing my ability to create advanced rendering techniques and visually compelling game experiences.


Here you can see some of the projects I've made as a College Project and out of curiosity. I have a strong perseverance for learning new things and taking on challenges, and one of my favorite part in game development is Level Design as it allows me to achieve the creative vision I have for games and allows me to design surreal levels. I've always been extremely curious in the technical side of games, and sometimes this curiosity has led me to projects I won't be able to formally mention here.

Putt in the Hole

C++ Ubisoft NEXT API Gameplay

The main goal is to putt the ball into the hole while earning enough points to complete each level. To boost your score, you can transform into different balls, each with unique abilities. However, every level has a random modifier that makes scoring more challenging. You’ll need to choose the right ball to counter the modifier, maximize your score, and finish the level.
